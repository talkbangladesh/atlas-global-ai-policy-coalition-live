## Source summary

I am a Ugandan lawyer, public policy professional and researcher working at the intersection of AI governance, law, human rights and public policy in Africa. I currently serve as an Assistant Legal Officer at the Uganda Law Society, where my work includes legal and policy advisory, professional ethics, compliance, legal reform and access to justice. I am also the Founder and CEO of Vine Legal Associates and Vine Tutoring Hub, where I lead initiatives focused on education, mentorship and professional development.

My professional and research interests increasingly focus on responsible AI and digital governance. I am a Full Member of the Law Society of African AI Professionals (LSAAP), a Research and Policy Fellow at the Centre for Policy Analysis (CEPA), and an Administrative Member of the International Institute for Strategic Research (IISR). I have also been involved with the International Society for Human Rights (ISHR), reflecting my broader commitment to human rights and accountable governance. My academic and professional development includes training in AI Security and Governance, AI Ethics, and the UNESCO Oxford Saïd programme on AI and Digital Transformation in Government.

My research interests include ethical AI governance in Africa, public sector AI, digital rights, human rights, technology policy, responsible innovation and inclusive development. I am particularly interested in how African countries can develop AI governance frameworks that are rights respecting, context sensitive and responsive to their own development priorities.

I would like to join the Atlas Global South AI Policy Coalition because I believe the future of AI governance must be shaped not only by technological and economic considerations, but also by law, human rights, development realities and the lived experiences of communities in the Global South. Coming from Uganda, I hope to contribute an African legal and policy perspective to conversations on responsible AI while learning from researchers, policymakers, civil society practitioners and institutions across the Global South.

I am particularly interested in contributing to collaborative research, policy discussions, working groups and knowledge products on AI governance in Africa. I see the Coalition as an opportunity to build meaningful cross border collaboration, strengthen evidence based AI policy, and help ensure that African countries are active contributors to global AI governance frameworks.

I would be honoured to contribute my legal, research and policy experience to the Coalition and to learn alongside others working to build more responsible, inclusive, rights respecting and development oriented AI governance.
