## Source summary

Dr. Obasesam Okoi is an Associate Professor of Justice and Peace Studies at the University of St. Thomas in Minnesota and Founding Director of the AI & Society Innovation Lab. His work focuses on AI governance, AI policy, responsible AI, and AI ethics, particularly their implications for societies in Africa and the Global South. He is interested in how governance frameworks can ensure that artificial intelligence advances human dignity, social justice, inclusive development, and the public good while addressing emerging technological risks and inequalities. Through interdisciplinary research, teaching, policy engagement, and the AI & Society Innovation Lab, he contributes to conversations on building human-centered and socially responsible approaches to AI governance.
