Senior Digital Media, AI & Fact-Checking Leader with 22+ years of experience in journalism, newsroom transformation, and technology-driven editorial operations. I have conceptualised and launched the framework “5W1H + 1E + 1B for AI” for newsrooms and journalism institutions. The model enables journalists to systematically assess What, Why, Who, When, Where, How, along with Ethics, Explainability, and Bias, when deploying AI in editorial workflows. The framework is actively used in newsrooms, academic settings, and professional training programmes to support responsible and transparent AI adoption.

My work focuses on AI-enabled newsroom workflows, misinformation and deepfake detection, digital verification systems, and editorial strategy at scale. My professional and academic associations include the London School of Economics (LSE), University of Oxford, Columbia Journalism School, East-West Center, and global media integrity networks such as the International Fact-Checking Network (IFCN) and Google News Initiative (GNI).
