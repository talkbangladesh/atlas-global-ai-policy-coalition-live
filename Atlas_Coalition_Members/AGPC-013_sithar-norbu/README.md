## Source summary

Sithar Norbu is a Chief Program Officer with the Public Service Delivery Division, Prime Minister’s Office, Royal Government of Bhutan. His work focuses on simplifying and standardizing government services, improving citizen journeys, strengthening inter-agency coordination, and expanding integrated service channels for citizens and businesses.

He holds an International Master’s in Artificial Intelligence for Public Services from Universidad Politécnica de Madrid and a Bachelor of Science (Honours) in Computer Science from the University of Delhi. His professional interests include citizen-centred digital government, GovTech, service design, digital inclusion, responsible AI and data governance.
