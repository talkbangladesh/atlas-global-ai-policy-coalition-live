## Source summary

Michael Bąk is a public policy, governance and technology leader with more than two decades of experience across government and diplomacy, international development, the United Nations, Big Tech, applied research and civil society. His career has focused on the intersection of technology, human rights, democratic governance, inclusion and public policy, including roles as Meta’s Head of Public Policy for Thailand and regional multilateral institutions, a senior UN Peace and Development Adviser, and a senior USAID governance adviser and diplomat.

Having lived most of his life in Asia and working primarily across the Global South, Michael brings a distinctive cross-sector perspective spanning policy development, research and analysis, strategic engagement and programme implementation. He has built coalitions across governments, technology companies, multilaterals, academia and civil society and has consistently championed the inclusion of diverse and historically underrepresented perspectives in policymaking. His current work and writing explore how AI and frontier technologies can be governed in ways that are more inclusive, accountable and grounded in Global South realities.
