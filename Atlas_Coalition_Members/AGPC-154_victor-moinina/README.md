## Source summary

Dr. Victor Moinina is a Sierra Leonean governance scholar, policy strategist and regional integration expert with more than a decade of experience in peacebuilding, institutional development and youth and gender empowerment across Africa.

He holds a PhD in Governance and Regional Integration (with Distinction) and an MSc in the same field from the Pan African University Institute for Governance, Humanities and Social Sciences, University of Yaoundé II, Cameroon. His first degree is a BA from Fourah Bay College, University of Sierra Leone.

His career began in peacebuilding as National Early Warning Systems Manager at WANEP-Sierra Leone, followed by a role as Programme Policy Officer with the World Food Programme in Ethiopia’s Somali Region, where he served as an African Union Youth Volunteer. Today he is Assistant Head of Department (Leadership and Development) and Lecturer at the Institute of Public Administration and Management (IPAM), University of Sierra Leone. He is also Executive Director and Lead Consultant of Pro-Idealist Limited, the research and consultancy firm he founded in 2021.

He chairs the board of Advocate for the Rights of Women and Girls Sierra Leone and serves as Policy and Research Lead at VIONet Sierra Leone. He has published peer-reviewed research on the green economy, energy security and civil society, and has supported a national civil society assessment of Sierra Leone’s progress on the SDGs.

His recognitions include the Pan African Youth Development Activist of the Year award and selection as Global Youth Ambassador for Sierra Leone. He is also an alumnus of the Mo Ibrahim Foundation Residential School on Governance and Development and the Young African Leaders Initiative (YALI), and has presented youth employment and Agenda 2063 outcomes to the African Union Commission.
