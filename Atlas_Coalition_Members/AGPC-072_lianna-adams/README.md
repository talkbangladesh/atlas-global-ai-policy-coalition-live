## Source summary

Lianna Adams is a Human-Centred AI Strategist, Digital Dignity Advocate, and Cognitive Culture Builder recognised by ForbesWeb3, Animoca Brands, and Open Campus as a 2024 Social Impact & Ethics leader and named one of the Top 10 Rising Women in Crypto 2023 including Best Women-led Startup to Watch 2025.

She has spoken at WEF, COP28, TEDx Youth, NFTNYC (four consecutive years), the World Web3.0 & AI Summit, EmpowHer AI, and Web3 Amsterdam, among others.

Her work centres on one urgent question: what do we preserve of ourselves as artificial intelligence transforms how we think, create, and relate? Through keynotes, workshops, and consulting, she helps organisations and communities build cultures where technology amplifies human capability without eroding cognitive autonomy, creative identity, or dignity.

She is the founder and creator of the Human-AI Cognitive Toolkit rooted in nine science-backed tools for cognitive wellness in an AI-saturated world and host of The Human Strata, a podcast and Substack essays exploring the human layer in the age of AI. She has hosted the Wirex Podcast and has convened 80,000+ community participants across digital events since 2021.

Lianna is also the founder of the Digital Dignity Initiative, leads the annual #16Days of Activism against Gender-Based Violence campaign, and has served as a Non-Executive Director at Mental Health Spaces Collective.

She has been part of UNESCO's Women in Science working group, participated at UN Open Source Week and a member of AI2030, a community working on Responsible AI.
