## Source summary

Rushikesh Kokare is a youth professional and researcher working at the intersection of public policy, education, international affairs, technology, and youth leadership. He holds a Postgraduate Diploma in Liberal Studies as a Young India Fellow at Ashoka University, with an interdisciplinary academic foundation in governance, international relations, public policy, and contemporary global affairs. He currently works as a Consultant with Teach For India and SCERT and is a Youth IGF India Research Fellow, working on issues including digital rights, AI governance, cybersecurity, data protection, and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) framework.

He has engaged with international and youth-focused platforms, including the World Bank Group Youth Summit as a delegate, UNICEF YuWaah Youth Advisory Board Member, the Better Future Forum, and BRICS-related engagements. He has also delivered a TED-style talk at Ashoka University. These experiences have strengthened his capabilities in policy research, international dialogue, communication, stakeholder engagement, and cross-cultural collaboration.

His interests span AI governance, digital rights, education, emerging technologies, technology and security, and public policy, with a particular interest in how technology can advance equitable access to education and opportunities. He is especially interested in ensuring that technology governance is inclusive, rights-respecting, evidence-based, and responsive to the realities and priorities of communities across the Global South.
