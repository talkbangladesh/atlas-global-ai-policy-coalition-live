## Source summary

I am a Brazilian social impact and innovation professional with more than 25 years of experience working at the intersection of socio-environmental development, inclusion, institutional transformation and, increasingly, artificial intelligence, in Brazil and in some African countries.
I am the co-founder of SUL – Inteligência para Impacto, a Brazil-based impact tech focused on developing and applying AI to social and environmental challenges in the Global South. My work combines AI literacy, responsible AI adoption, knowledge development and the design of AI solutions for organizations and communities working in contexts of social and economic inequality.
Through SUL, I co-lead the development of Mariah, an AI-based educational and business support platform designed to expand access to knowledge, capabilities and decision support for nano and microentrepreneurs, particularly those underserved by conventional technology and business support systems. Mariah combines education, conversational AI and practical business support to make technology more accessible and useful in everyday economic life.
We are also developing the AI Brain for Impact, a knowledge infrastructure designed to organize and connect curated knowledge, support knowledge management and enable collaborative knowledge creation across the social impact ecosystem. The goal is not simply to make information searchable, but to create an intelligent layer through which people and organizations can access, connect, interpret and collectively develop knowledge for action.
My work is grounded in the belief that responsible AI in the Global South must go beyond principles and regulation to address questions of access, agency, capacity, inclusion and real-world impact. I hold a Master's degree in International Relations and Development Studies and MBAs in Entrepreneurship & Business Management and Human Resources Development.
