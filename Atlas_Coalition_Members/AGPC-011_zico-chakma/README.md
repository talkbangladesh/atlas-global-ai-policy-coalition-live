## Source summary

Zico Chakma is a Communication and Journalism graduate from the University of Chittagong, Bangladesh, and is currently pursuing his M.S.S. in Communication and Journalism at the same institution. He is a communication professional with a particular focus on Indigenous storytelling, documentary storytelling, and community-centered communication.

As a youth fellow at ICCAD, Zico is engaged in exploring emerging issues at the intersection of communication, technology, and society. His interests extend to the Indigenous and environmental dimensions of AI policy and governance, particularly how emerging technologies can be developed and governed in ways that are inclusive, ethical, and responsive to Indigenous communities and environmental concerns.

Through storytelling, research, and youth engagement, he aims to amplify underrepresented voices and contribute to more inclusive approaches to communication, technology, and policymaking.
