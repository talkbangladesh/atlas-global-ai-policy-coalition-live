Dr. Wafa Al-Daily is a global executive and strategic advisor with over 25 years of experience in governance, international development, urban transformation, and institutional leadership. Her professional interests lie at the intersection of AI governance, digital transformation, international cooperation, and equitable development, particularly the implications of emerging technologies for institutional capacity, human rights, and vulnerable communities.
