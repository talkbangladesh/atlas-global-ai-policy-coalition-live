## Source summary

I am an electrical engineer, philosopher, and scholar of diplomacy, and a Senior Member of IEEE, with more than 25 years of international experience across North America, Europe, Latin America, and the Caribbean. My work sits at the intersection of AI governance, ethics, human rights, education, and public policy.

I have contributed to international AI governance standards, including IEEE P3462 on Safety by Design in Generative Models for Child Safety, IEEE 7003 on Algorithmic Bias Considerations, and IEEE P2863 on Organizational Governance of AI. I have also served on the IEEE Global Initiative Education Committee, was selected by UNESCO to deliver a Master Class on AI and Digital Transformation for the launch of its International Research Centre on Artificial Intelligence, and serve as a Founding Editorial Board Member of Springer Nature’s AI and Ethics journal.

I founded GovAI Kids™ and developed Governance Readiness Before Design™, an approach focused on establishing evidence of governance readiness before AI design, pilot, or deployment when children may be affected. My broader work explores how AI governance can become more anticipatory, evidence-based, and responsive to the diverse realities of communities and institutions globally, including those in the Global South.
