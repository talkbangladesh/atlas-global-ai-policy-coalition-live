## Source summary

Nuraini Usman is a data scientist, academic, researcher, and technology professional from Nigeria with over seven years of experience spanning data science and analytics, information technology, data governance, internet governance, cybersecurity, and digital transformation. He holds a Master of Technology in Data Science and Analytics from Universiti Teknikal Malaysia Melaka and a Bachelor of Information Technology from the International University of East Africa. He currently serves as a Lecturer II in Computer Science at the Federal University of Technology, Babura, where he combines teaching, research, and institutional research and innovation activities. His research interests include responsible and explainable artificial intelligence, machine learning, data governance, digital inclusion, AI policy, and emerging technology governance. He also serves as a Regional Coordinator for the Internet Society Nigeria Chapter, supporting initiatives on internet governance, digital rights, data governance, and inclusive digital development. His research and publications have examined AI, machine learning, DNS policy, big data, digital security, and technology applications in healthcare.
