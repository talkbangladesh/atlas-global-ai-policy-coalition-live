## Source summary

Gina Grant, M.Ed., is Founder and CEO of CoreKind, a human-centered innovation studio focused on responsible AI and the future of learning and work. With more than 20 years of experience across education, workforce development, and social innovation, she has helped institutions build the capacity to adopt emerging technologies in ways that center human dignity, strengthen agency, and expand equitable access to opportunity.
Her work bridges human-centered design, responsible technology, and institutional transformation, with a particular focus on ensuring that the people and communities most affected by technological change have a meaningful voice in shaping it. Her experience spans education, community-based innovation, and workforce development, including digital literacy initiatives for justice-impacted learners.
In November 2026, she will participate as a panelist at the Africa Celebrates Women Forum in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, contributing to conversations on women's leadership, innovation, and the future of African and global communities.
