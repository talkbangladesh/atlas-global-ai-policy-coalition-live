## Source summary

I am a researcher and Assistant Professor of Geography at Hemvati Nandan Bahuguna Garhwal University, Uttarakhand, India. My work focuses on population geography, spatial demography, health, elections, and the use of geospatial technologies and GeoAI for evidence-based research and public decision-making. I am particularly interested in how AI can be applied responsibly to address real-world challenges in communities across the Global South.

Through my research, teaching, and open mapping initiatives, I work at the intersection of geography, data, technology, and society. I am keen to contribute to the Atlas Global South AI Policy Coalition by sharing perspectives from India, learning from researchers and practitioners across the Global South, and supporting inclusive, ethical, and context-sensitive approaches to AI governance.
