## Source summary

Adv. Lufuno T. Tshikalange (LLM, Chartered CIO) is a South African multidisciplinary and award-winning Cyberlaw, ICT Governance and AI Governance expert with over 20 years of experience spanning technology, law, data protection and privacy, supply chain and ICT contract management, digital policy, cybersecurity GRC, and sustainability, including ESG and Green IT. Her career encompasses public-sector leadership, national and international technology standards, AI policy and governance, digital transformation, and strategic oversight of emerging technologies. Her mission is to facilitate Africa’s meaningful participation in the global digital economy through resilient, rights-respecting, secure, inclusive and context responsive digital transformation.

She is the Founder and Executive Director of Orizur Consulting Enterprise, an award-winning cyberlaw and digital governance consultancy. Under her leadership, Orizur has developed as a specialised consultancy providing strategic advisory services to public- and private-sector clients on complex digital governance and regulatory challenges, helping to bridge the business-IT gap, strengthen data and AI governance, operationalise compliance, and enhance cyber resilience. The consultancy has been recognised with the Global Leadership in Digital Governance & Cyber Resilience 2026 award.

She is currently one of seven members of South Africa's National AI Policy Expert Review Panel, providing expert contributions to national AI policy, and serves as Chairperson of SABS/TC 0001/SC27, an ISO/IEC Mirror Committee on Information Security. She also participates in SABS technical committees on IT Service Management and IT Governance and Artificial Intelligence, connecting national standards development with international standards and emerging approaches to AI and information security governance.

Adv. Tshikalange previously served as Acting Director: Transversal Contracts and Cost Management in the Department of Public Service and Administration, where she led the restructuring of government ICT sourcing strategies to create a level playing field for economic inclusion, particularly for MSMEs, while embedding security by design and Green IT into the management of ICT transversal contracts. This experience provided her with extensive exposure to public-sector procurement, ICT governance, regulatory compliance, strategic sourcing, economic and digital inclusion, and the management of complex ICT environments.

Her professional and policy engagement extends from national institutions to continental and international platforms. She has contributed to discussions relating to the African Union AI Strategy, WSIS+20 Review, and the UN Global Digital Compact and has participated in international AI standards and policy engagements. Her work brings together AI governance, digital rights, cybersecurity, data governance, technology standards and institutional capacity, with an emphasis on governance approaches that are context-based and appropriate to the social, economic and institutional realities of Africa and the wider Global South.

Her governance experience includes serving as Deputy Chairperson of the Critical Infrastructure Council, as a member of public-sector Audit and ICT Governance Committees, and as a Council Member of South West Gauteng TVET College. Her work has received recognition, including the Outstanding Woman in Tech Award, Africa (2024), recognition among the Top 50 African Women in Cybersecurity and Compliance (2025), and the Top 20 Personalities in Cybersecurity, Africa (2021).
