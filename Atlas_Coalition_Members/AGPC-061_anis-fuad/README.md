## Source summary

Anis Fuad is a researcher and lecturer in public health informatics and digital health at Universitas Gadjah Mada, Indonesia. With more than 15 years of experience, he has worked with government agencies, international organizations, healthcare providers, and professional associations on digital health policy, health information systems, interoperability, data governance, regulatory innovation, and responsible AI in health. He serves on the Governing Council of the Asia eHealth Information Network and leads the Data and Information division of the Indonesian Hospital Association. Trained in medicine and medical informatics, he has authored more than 40 international scientific publications. His current interests include responsible AI governance, AI-enabled public health surveillance, digital health regulation, and strengthening institutional capacity for equitable digital transformation in low- and middle-income countries.
