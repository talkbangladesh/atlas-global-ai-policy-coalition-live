## Source summary

Victor Prasad Karunan, PhD, is a Visiting Professor with the Indian Sociological Society (ISS), based in Bangkok. He has more than 35 years of professional experience in over 40 countries across the Global North and Global South, with a particular focus on South Asia, Southeast Asia and the Pacific.

He spent over 16 years with UNICEF, serving as Regional Advisor on Participation and Partnerships at the Regional Office in Bangkok, Chief of Adolescent Development and Participation at Headquarters in New York, and Representative/Deputy Representative and Senior Social Policy Specialist in UNICEF Malaysia. He was also Regional Development Advisor for Save the Children-UK at its Regional Office for Southeast Asia and the Pacific in Bangkok.

His work supports governments, United Nations agencies, international development organisations, NGOs, civil society and the private sector in child rights and child development, results-based management, social policy, public finance management, adolescent participation, policy advocacy, participatory research, and training and capacity development. He has also worked as a Development Evaluation Consultant for European donor agencies and has led research, evaluations and training programmes with community organisations and NGOs in over 15 countries in South and Southeast Asia. He holds an M.A. in Sociology from Jawaharlal Nehru University, New Delhi, and a PhD in the Social Sciences from the University of Nijmegen, the Netherlands.
