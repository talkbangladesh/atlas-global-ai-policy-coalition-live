## Source summary

I hold an MSc in Digital Society at the University of Edinburgh under the Chevening Award, a postgraduate degree in Business Administration from FGV, and a degree in International Relations. I currently work as a Project Management Consultant for the UK Government’s Digital Access Programme (DAP), supporting the Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office (FCDO). In this role, I manage projects focused on digital technologies, artificial intelligence, digital policy, digital safety, and meaningful connectivity.

Throughout my career, I have worked with several international organisations and leading institutions, including the University of Cambridge, Microsoft, The Ohio State University, and Facebook (now Meta). At Meta, I served as a Programme Manager.

At Microsoft, I also served as the leader of BAM in Brazil, and Business Manager for the External and Corporate Affairs department connecting digital technology and policy. At Microsft I developed strategies and initiatives aimed at creating a more diverse and inclusive environment within the technology sector. My work focused on professional development, self-awareness, networking, and advancing discussions on racial equity in Brazil. I also created initiatives to raise awareness of digital exclusion in Brazil.

As a consultant to the British Government’s Digital Access Programme, I have managed several projects focused on the intersection of technology, public policy, and social impact. One example is the “Digital Technologies and Environmental Sustainability in Brazil” project, which focused on mapping and disseminating research on the use of artificial intelligence to support environmental sustainability while addressing and mitigating the risks associated with its adoption. 

Another example is a Digital Citizenship project implemented in collaboration with SaferNet and Brazil’s Ministry of Education. I played an instrumental role in designing and implementing digital safety and citizenship courses that reached 145,548 students across 1,065 schools in all 27 Brazilian states. I co-wrote the project proposal, managed its implementation, and ensured alignment with both Brazilian and UK Government priorities. By supporting the integration of digital safety initiatives into public policy, I contributed to significant nationwide impact, demonstrating my ability to manage large-scale projects and navigate complex international collaborations.
