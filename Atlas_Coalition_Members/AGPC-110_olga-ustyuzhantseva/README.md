## Source summary

My work focuses on AI governance in the Global South, particularly on how institutional capacity, evidence practices, digital infrastructure and technological dependencies shape policy choices and implementation. At the University of Johannesburg, I conduct research on AI governance and broader processes of technological and institutional change, while through OLAM Africa Research Institute I lead policy-oriented work on emerging technologies. I am interested in joining the Coalition to contribute African perspectives to comparative research, exchange evidence and policy experience across regions, and collaborate on joint work that can support more context-sensitive and institutionally grounded approaches to AI governance.
