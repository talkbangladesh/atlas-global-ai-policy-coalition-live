## Source summary

R. Verity Vabolis is the Director and Research Lead at Hitherto AI, Flux Hall Academy and Fathom Lab, as well as the founder and director of the Hitherto Center for the Advancement of Technological Trust, SCIO (H-CATT), an independent nonprofit dedicated to rebuilding technological trust from the architecture up. Her work spans sovereign digital infrastructure, anti-extractive, procurement standards, and the design of partitioned, domain-specific AI systems for public institutions, particularly across the Global South. 
Her background is deliberately non-linear: 9 years in medicine, a CEO role in Consumer Goods, leadership of a human services nonprofit spanning 18 counties, before a near fatal car accident in 2016 redirected her toward AI. She has worked in the industry since 2018, and now heads H-CATTs research into architectural enforcement, data sovereignty, and the conditions under which AI can serve the common good, rather than extract from it. 
She is the author of Kairos Praxis, a field guide for those who refuse to settle for what is put in front of them.
