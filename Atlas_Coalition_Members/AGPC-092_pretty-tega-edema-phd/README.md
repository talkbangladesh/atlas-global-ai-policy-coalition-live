## Source summary

I work around the intersection of Education, AI governance, AI ethics/responsible AI deployment particularly within the Global South. I am currently the Research Lead at Pan African Centre for AI Ethics (PACFAIE) and an AI Ethics and Governance Fellow of the Policy and Innovation Centre (PiC). As my capstone project, I just developed an Audit Checklist  for AI Proctoring and Grading Assessment Tools to be used within Sub sahara Africa. I also have a soon-to-be-published monograph titled, Artificial Intelligence in African Context: An Overview of Key Issues which critically examines salient and pivotal factors to be considered in relation to AI investment, deployment and policymaking in Africa. I am also the author of four books on data privacy and protection.
