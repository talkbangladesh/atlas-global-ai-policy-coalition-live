## Source summary

Gabriel Hidalgo Andrade is a political scientist, constitutional lawyer, and university professor based in Quito, Ecuador, specializing in political power, governance, and institutional conflict in Latin America. With over 15 years of undergraduate and graduate teaching experience, his research and teaching focus on applied political science, Latin American presidentialism, political elites, and institutional stability. He runs professional training programs in political analysis and public governance for public-sector officials and advisors, and is a regular commentator in national and international media. In 2026, he co-founded E3I (Escenarios · Información · Incidencia · Inteligencia Estratégica), a strategic intelligence organization producing political risk analysis across Ecuador and the region.
