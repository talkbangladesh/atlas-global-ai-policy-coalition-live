## Source summary

Major Vineet Kumar is a globally recognized leader in cyber diplomacy and cyber peacebuilding who has redefined cybersecurity as a force for social good. As Founder and Global President of CyberPeace, he has spent nearly three decades building one of the world’s largest citizen driven movements for online safety, inclusion, and digital resilience, impacting more than 200 million people across 112 countries.

An alumnus of Cranfield University, GCSP Geneva, and the University of Cambridge, Vineet is a Chevening Fellow of the UK Government and an alumnus of the U.S. Department of State’s International Visitor Leadership Program. He has also participated in the British Council Future Leaders Programme 2017. He is associated with prestigious academic institutions as an Adjunct Faculty Member and Professor of Practice. As a subject matter expert, he has contributed to United Nations, government, and parliamentary committees, consultations, and expert commentaries on cybersecurity, AI safety, cyber policy, and digital trust.

He leads global collaborations with the United Nations, G20, Commonwealth Secretariat, ITU, ICANN, leading technology platforms, and other international institutions, helping shape global norms on AI safety, cybersecurity, and digital trust. He has trained diplomats and senior government officials from more than 100 countries, authored multiple acclaimed books, published several IEEE papers, and holds multiple patents in cybersecurity and AI safety.

A recipient of 13 international and 25 national awards, Vineet’s recognitions include Entrepreneur Magazine’s Social Entrepreneur of the Year 2025, The Times of India Social Entrepreneur of the Year 2025, and the National Youth Award 2014 to 2015, the Government of India’s highest youth honour. He was also recognized as a Finalist for the World Economic Forum Social Entrepreneur of the Year Award in 2021, nominated as a Young Global Leader Class of 2026, and named a Semi Finalist for Forbes 30 Under 30 Asia in 2018.

He embodies the mission of using technology to empower people, strengthen peace, and build a trusted digital future for humanity. He has been a distinguished speaker at leading global forums, including the United Nations General Assembly, UN Security Council Counter Terrorism Committee, UN Internet Governance Forum, ITU WSIS Forum, Global Cybersecurity Forum, UN Cybercrime Convention negotiations, SPIEF 2025, the Responsible AI in the Military Summit in South Korea, and the Valdai Discussion Club Annual Forum.

His work and thought leadership have been featured by leading international and national media outlets, including Forbes, TIME, BBC, CNN, Channel 4, TechCrunch, Mashable, Radio France Internationale, India Today, Times Now, NDTV, ANI, and Rajya Sabha TV.
