## Source summary

Carl Sherson Clermont is a Haitian digital transformation leader, AI policy and governance practitioner, and CEO & Principal Consultant of CompuConsult. With over two decades of experience at the intersection of technology, public policy, and international development, his work focuses on responsible AI, digital government, digital public infrastructure, cybersecurity, and inclusive innovation. He leads initiatives including SMARTSCHOOL and MÈT-DESCH, a Trusted Educational AI Agent designed to expand responsible and equitable access to AI-powered learning. Carl contributes to regional dialogue on AI governance and digital transformation across Latin America and the Caribbean, with a particular interest in ensuring that Haiti, Small Island Developing States, and other Global South countries actively shape the policies, safeguards, and institutions governing AI.
