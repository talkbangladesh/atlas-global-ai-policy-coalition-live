## Source summary

Rais Nouman Ahmed is an Assistant Professor of Law at Bahauddin Zakariya University, Multan, with fifteen years of teaching experience in constitutional law, criminal law, and technology regulation. He holds an LLM from Staffordshire University, England, UK and is doctorate on Regulating Artificial Intelligence to Develop its Legislative Framework for Pakistan, one of the first sustained domestic studies of AI regulation grounded in Pakistani constitutional doctrine. His work examines algorithmic accountability, data governance, and due process under Article 10A of the Constitution of Pakistan 1973. He writes regularly on AI regulation for national and professional audiences.
