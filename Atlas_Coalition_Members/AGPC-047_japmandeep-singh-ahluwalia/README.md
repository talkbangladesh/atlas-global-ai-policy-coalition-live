## Source summary

Japmandeep Singh Ahluwalia, known as Sunny, is an AI governance practitioner based in Gurgaon, India and the founder of Luxyn Ethics and Governance. His work focuses on the gritty operational side of AI safety, specifically accountability triggers, which are hard system checkpoints that force human review before high risk AI decisions are executed. He holds an Oxford University AI Ethics certification and the BSI ISO 42001 Lead Implementer designation, with recent publications on verifiable governance frameworks and the 2026 audit landscape.

He is currently building an EU AI Act Classification Simulator to help organisations operationalise compliance across jurisdictions. He wants to join the Coalition to test this framework against the regulatory realities of the Global South, share practical implementation blueprints, and learn from peers facing the same infrastructure and capacity constraints that define the region's AI trajectory. His focus is always on what works on the ground, not just what looks good in a policy paper.
