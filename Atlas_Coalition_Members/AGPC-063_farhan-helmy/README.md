## Source summary

Farhan Helmy is Founder and CEO of ASCODI Lab, working at the intersection of systems thinking, AI, geospatial intelligence, climate and sustainability, and inclusive development. With more than three decades of experience in environmental and climate policy, geospatial analysis, systems approaches, and multi-stakeholder governance, his work increasingly focuses on how AI and emerging computational technologies can strengthen public decision-making while remaining accountable to communities. He previously contributed to Indonesia’s climate-policy processes, including engagement in UNFCCC negotiations. His current work explores community-centered AI governance, responsible and participatory data systems, civic sense-making, system dynamics and agent-based modelling, with particular attention to the Global South and to people and communities often underrepresented in conventional data, technology, and policy processes.
