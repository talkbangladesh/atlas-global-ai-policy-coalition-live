## Source summary

Nafiul Ahmad Rafi is an AI governance researcher and policy architect based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, and Founding Director of Atlas AI Institute, a Global South AI governance and policy research initiative. His research examines the governance infrastructure and regulatory capacity needed for responsible AI adoption in developing and emerging economies, with an emphasis on institutional readiness and equitable participation in international norm-setting forums. Through Atlas AI Institute he advances Global South perspectives in multilateral AI governance processes, and his work spans regulatory capacity assessment, comparative Global South AI policy analysis (including country-level AI policy and governance SWOT frameworks), and the link between governance research and digital inclusion and civic technology. He also serves as National AI Policy Endorser at the AI Policy Lab, Umeå University, Sweden, and is a member of the CIVICUS policy network, the Internet Society and the Responsible AI Hub.
