## Source summary

Yalilé Loaiza is an investigative journalist based in Quito, Ecuador, with over 15 years of experience in journalism and media innovation. She is Editor-in-Chief of Ecuador Chequea, the only fact-checking organization in Ecuador that is a signatory to the IFCN Code of Principles , and leads ChequeaLab, its digital innovation arm developing AI-assisted verification tools. She also serves as President of Fundamedios, Ecuador's leading press freedom organization, and is the Ecuador correspondent for Infobae since 2021. In 2025, she co-founded E3I (Escenarios · Información · Incidencia · Inteligencia Estratégica), a strategic intelligence organization producing political risk analysis and training journalists across Ecuador. She holds a Summa Cum Laude master's degree in Digital Communication Research from Universidad de Los Hemisferios.
