## Source summary

Lu Yan is a computer scientist and infrastructure engineer whose career spans academic research and UK government IT. He holds a PhD in Computer Science from Åbo Akademi University, Finland, and a BSc from Peking University. Over a decade of internationally funded research in formal methods, verification, and dependable systems, he produced 49 peer-reviewed publications, served as Principal Investigator on an Academy of Finland grant, and held appointments at University College London, the University of Cambridge, and the University of Hertfordshire. He is currently an Infrastructure Engineer at the UK Department for Education, operating the Azure platform underpinning national education services. His recent training spans AI engineering, agentic systems, and public policy at Oxford and LSE. His interests centre on verification, evaluation, and dependability in AI governance.
