## Source summary

Olaolu Oluwasanmi’s research focuses on international migration, diaspora engagement, diaspora studies, and urban development, particularly in relation to state agency, belonging, and citizenship. His recent article, Diaspora Vote: Relationship and Power of the Legislature in Political Leadership in Nigeria, was published in African Identities, a Taylor & Francis journal. He has organised panels and presented his work at both local and international conferences, actively contributing to scholarly discourse. Oluwasanmi organised a panel at the Lagos Studies Association (LSA) 2025 Conference titled “Navigating Identities: Migration, Belonging, and Identity Formation.” In recognition of his academic contributions, he received the LSA Travel Grant and the UKRI Publication Grant in 2024. Additionally, he was awarded a travel grant to participate in the CERTIZENS West Africa Regional Workshop in Accra, Ghana, in April 2025. In June 2025, he participated in the LSA Writing Workshop through a grant. He also holds a Non-Violence and Peace Fellowship from the Sri Jamnalal Bajaj Memorial Library and Research Centre for Gandhian Studies (JBMLRCGS), Sevagram Ashram Pratishthan, Wardha, Maharashtra, India.
