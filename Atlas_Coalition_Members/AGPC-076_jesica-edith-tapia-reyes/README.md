## Source summary

Jésica Tapia is an AI policy researcher, consultant, and AI developer with more than 15 years of experience spanning the public sector, academia, and applied AI. Her work sits at the intersection of AI policy and regulation, AI ethics and safety, and cooperative AI, with a sustained focus on gender and AI governance.

She is a member of CAIDP's AI Policy Group (Global Majority track) and of UNESCO's Women4Ethical AI leadership group, and a contributing author to CAIDP's Artificial Intelligence and Democratic Values Index. As CTO of Analítica Boutique, she designs and deploys applied AI systems — multi-agent architectures, RAG, NLP, and computer vision — and presented "Human-Centered AI Design and Inclusive Innovation" at an endorsed side event of the 2026 Global Dialogue on AI Governance.

She teaches at UNAM and UVM, coordinates and lectures in the Diploma in Data Science for the Social Sciences and Humanities at FES Acatlán–UNAM (seven cohorts since 2022), and co-founded IA para Mujeres, a program on the gender pay gap and women's leadership in generative AI.She is Claude Community Ambassador in México City for Social Sciences and Humanities.
