## Source summary

Ed.M. candidate in International Education Policy and Management at Harvard Graduate School of Education. With nearly two decades of experience as a teacher, education leader, and policy practitioner across Türkiye and the United States, my research focuses on education equity for marginalized populations and the policy reforms needed to ensure systems reach the people they are designed to serve. I previously served as the sole statewide Education Specialist for an agency supporting individuals with disabilities across 39 NYS counties and as a Regional Manager overseeing early childhood education centers. I was recently appointed to the NYS Commissioner's Advisory Panel for Special Education Services, advising the Governor, Legislature, and Commissioner on unmet needs for students with disabilities statewide. I hold an International Teacher Certificate from Cambridge and ECIS. Published under Professor Fernando M. Reimers. Bilingual researcher conducting primary research in Turkish and English.
