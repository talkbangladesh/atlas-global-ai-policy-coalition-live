## Source summary

Elizabeth Ballesteros is the founder of IGM Human Systems, an emerging research and innovation initiative examining how artificial intelligence affects the formation, exercise and preservation of human capabilities. Her work focuses particularly on human agency, judgment, responsibility and self-trust—and on the developmental environments through which these capacities are built over time.
IGM is developing a human capability framework, research architecture and applied assessment approaches intended to help education systems, organizations and policymakers distinguish between AI-assisted performance and independently possessed human capability. Elizabeth’s wider interests include human development, equitable access to developmental opportunity and the design of human-development-centred AI systems and institutions.
