Uloma Okoro is the Africa Lead at the Aula Fellowship for AI, where she supports regional engagement, policy dialogue, research, and partnerships across Africa’s AI governance community. Uloma has spoken at legal and professional forums, including the Legal Gorilla Virtual Conference, and has delivered responsible AI training to journalists and other professional groups. She is also a senior corporate and commercial lawyer with over 20 years of experience and currently heads Legal Services and Compliance in the fintech and payments sector. Her work connects AI policy with institutional governance, regulatory compliance, technology contracts, and accountability.
