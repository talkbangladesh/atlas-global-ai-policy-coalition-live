## Source summary

Dr. Wafa Al-Daily is an international urban development, planning, governance, and sustainable development expert and Executive Director and Co-Founder of EHAAR International LLC. She holds a Ph.D. in Planning, Governance, and Globalization from Virginia Tech and has more than two decades of experience spanning academia, international development, policy, institutional capacity building, and urban and regional development.
Her work has involved collaboration with international organizations, development institutions, universities, governments, and professional networks, with particular experience in the Global South and fragile and conflict-affected settings. Her areas of expertise include urban governance, sustainable development, post-conflict recovery and reconstruction, resilience, education, institutional capacity development, gender-responsive planning, accessibility, and inclusive development.
Her growing work on artificial intelligence focuses on its implications for governance, cities, education, public institutions, sustainable development, and societies experiencing fragility and inequality. She is particularly interested in ensuring that Global South perspectives and local realities are meaningfully represented in emerging global AI governance frameworks.
