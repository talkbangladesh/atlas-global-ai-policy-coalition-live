## Source summary

Radhika Venkataraman is a Staff UX Researcher working on AI trust and safety, based in Bengaluru. She brings 25 years of qualitative research across consumer insights, ethnography and UX, and teaches qualitative methods and cultural strategy at MICA. Her current research looks at how AI systems handle non-Western English, using Indian English as the primary case, and at where cultural bias enters the evaluation and tooling layer rather than the model itself. She is a part-time PhD candidate and mentors early-career researchers through WIRE and upGrad. She also writes essays and criticism at primalsoup.in, with work published in the New York Times, Out of Print and Punch.
