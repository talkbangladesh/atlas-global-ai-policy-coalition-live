## Source summary

Mohsena Akter Drishty is an AI governance researcher and policy analyst based in Bangladesh, with seven years of experience in areas of responsible technology, digital rights, and regulatory analysis. She has conducted country-level AI governance assessments for the Center for AI and Digital Policy (CAIDP) and has been promoted to Team Leader for the Fall 2026 Advanced Clinic cohort. Her earlier work includes producing 250+ legal research outputs for organizations, non-profits, law firms and academics.

She publishes The Harm Index, an independent AI policy publication on Substack covering algorithmic accountability, digital rights, and technology-facilitated harm in South Asia. She also serves as a Founding Board Member for AI Ethics and Standards at Am I Tech Enough Ltd. in Dubai.
