## Source summary

As Chair, I have steered the SIG's strategic direction under the defining theme of "Ethics and Governance in the Era of Ubiquitous AI." This was not an abstract intellectual exercise but a practical response to what I observed among information professionals globally. I was driven by a widespread uncertainty about engaging with AI systems ethically, a lack of institutional policy frameworks, and an urgent need for accessible governance tools. Under my leadership, SIG-IEP launched the Student Research Award on AI Governance, a $1,000 annual grant designed to fund student-led projects that advance transparent and responsible AI oversight. The inaugural award was won by Mary Anne Kochenderfer of San José State University, whose project,  "Generative AI for Libraries", is developing a publicly accessible platform to build AI governance literacy among library professionals.
