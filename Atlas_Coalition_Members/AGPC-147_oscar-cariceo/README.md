## Source summary

Oscar Cariceo Rivera is a Software Engineer, Anthropologist, and Master of Social Work with over 12 years of experience bridging technical AI development, regulatory compliance, and human-centered design in the Global South. As the Founder of KintuAI, he builds applied Explainable AI (XAI) solutions and governance frameworks that make algorithmic decisions auditable, transparent, and fair. Concurrently, as UX Research Manager at Scotiabank Chile, he leads cross-functional teams to evaluate and mitigate the human, operational, and adoption risks of automated systems in highly regulated financial environments. A selected Fellow of the BID fAIr-LAC initiative, Oscar specializes in translating abstract AI governance principles into practical, operational controls, ensuring that AI deployment in Latin America is equitable, traceable, and aligned with both local regulatory mandates and global responsible AI standards.
