## Source summary

I am the Founder and Executive Director of the Responsible AI Governance Initiative (RAI-GI), an independent public-interest nonprofit building the institutional capacity Africa needs to govern artificial intelligence safely, accountably, and in the public interest.

My work focuses on one of the defining governance challenges of our time: ensuring that governments, regulators, civil society, and industry possess the expertise, systems, and partnerships required to translate AI governance principles into effective institutional practice.

Through RAI-GI, I lead initiatives that strengthen AI governance through independent research, practical decision-maker training, multi-stakeholder partnerships, and implementation-focused policy support. Our work helps bridge the gap between AI governance ambition and institutional reality, enabling African institutions to adopt and govern advanced AI responsibly while protecting the public interest.

I believe Africa should not only participate in global conversations on AI governance but help shape them. The continent's experience, institutions, and perspectives are essential to building governance frameworks that are effective, inclusive, and globally relevant.

My long-term ambition is to contribute to building trusted institutions capable of governing increasingly powerful AI systems while positioning Africa as a leading voice in the future of global AI governance.
