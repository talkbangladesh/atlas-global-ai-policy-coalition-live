## Source summary

Imoh Ebenezer is the Founder of Datariom Core, a company dedicated to helping small and medium enterprises (SMEs) adopt Artificial Intelligence (AI) to drive growth, efficiency, and innovation. With a strong foundation in AI literacy, research commercialization, and technology-driven solutions, he operates at the intersection of innovation and real-world impact. 

Through Datariom Core, Imoh advances the belief that AI should not remain a distant concept for African businesses, but a practical tool for solving pressing challenges—from streamlining operations to unlocking new market opportunities. Central to this work is nForce, Datariom Core’s AI-powered platform for real-time fraud detection, risk monitoring, and automated AML compliance, designed specifically for financial institutions and agent-based networks. 

Through nForce, he is building systems that move organizations from reactive operations to proactive, intelligence-driven decision-making. 

Beyond Datariom Core, Imoh served as Chief Data Officer at the Grassroots Movement for Bola Ahmed Tinubu, where he leads data-driven strategy and analytics across Nigeria. He is also a contributor to the Nigeria Economic Summit Group (NESG), supporting the Policy Commission in advancing national conversations on technology, innovation, and economic transformation. 

At his core, Imoh sees Africa as a continent defined by creativity, resilience, and untapped potential. He believes the next wave of global innovation can emerge from Africa if young innovators are equipped with the right tools, mentorship, and access to technology. His vision is to position Africa not only as a participant in the global digital economy, but as a driver of it.
