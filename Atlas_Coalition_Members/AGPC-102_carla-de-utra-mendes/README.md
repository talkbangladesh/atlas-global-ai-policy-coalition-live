## Source summary

Carla de Utra Mendes was a Postdoctoral Research Associate in China and Mental Health at the Lau China Institute, King’s College London, UK.  She worked mainly on emerging and digital technologies and mental health in China, writing a policy paper on the matter. She is now a Scientific Officer at FCT Foundation for Science and Technology in Portugal and was pursuing a second PhD in Global Public Health at Escola Nacional de Saúde Pública of NOVA University of Lisbon with a focus on Global Mental Health. 

Carla holds a PhD in Global Studies ( awarded by the University of Saint Joseph, Macao S.A.R., China and Catholic University, Portugal), as former fellow of the Foundation for Science and Technology (FCT) of Portugal. Graduate in Art History, and since her Masters degree, both from the Faculty of Human and Social Sciences of Universidade NOVA Lisbon, Portugal, she has been focusing on East Asia, particularly on Chinese Studies. She is currently completing an MA in Psychoanalytic Studies at Tavistock and Portman NHS Foundation Trust in London, UK. 

Carla has developed research on contemporary China with interests that range from politics to art and cultural analysis and emerging technologies.  Some of her publications include China Daily, co-authoring with Professor Kerry Brown ‘China’s vision of common destiny of a shared future’ (2018) and, individually ‘Xi’s speech lays out positive vision’ (2018). She is also a member of the British Association for Chinese Studies (BACS) and the Chinese Collections Group in the UK.

 In the field of contemporary art and museums, she has fulfilled several roles from museum and independent curator, museum educator and independent art critic, having collaborated with institutions such as the Freud Museum, London,  Calouste Gulbenkian Foundation and Culturgest.
