## Source summary

I am Dr.Belay Enyew Chekol and an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at the University of Gondar, Ethiopia, specializing in Fuzzy Logic Systems, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data and Web Mining and Intelligent Systems. I have a PhD in Computer Science from Sudan University of Science and Technology, an MCA from Osmania University, India, and a BSc in Library and Information Science from Addis Ababa University.
I have extensive experience in university teaching, postgraduate supervision, academic program coordination, curriculum development, research, and community engagement. He has served as Postgraduate Programs Coordinator in the College of Informatics and previously as Head of the Department of Information Technology at the University of Gondar.
My research interests focus on the application of AI and machine learning to real-world challenges, including disease prediction, intelligent systems, data mining, fuzzy logic, deep learning, digital transformation, and AI-enabled decision support. My research includes work on malaria epidemic prediction, agricultural disease detection, medical image analysis, and AI-related security and governance.
I am particularly interested in advancing responsible, practical, and context-aware AI solutions for developing countries, with an emphasis on translating AI research into applications that can contribute to health, agriculture, public services, education, and sustainable development.
